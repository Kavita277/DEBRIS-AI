Why DEBRIS?

End-to-End Circular Economy Ecosystem

DEBRIS (Digital Eco-Bin Robotics & Intelligent System) bridges the gap between citizens, municipal authorities, and recycling facilities. Our platform provides automated sorting guides, real-time bin locations, and analytical insights.

  • ✓ Automated classification reduces contamination in recycling bins by 82%.
  • ✓ Dynamic truck routing reduces municipal fuel consumption and CO₂ emissions.
  • ✓ Reverse Vending Machine (RVM) integration rewards instant cashbacks & bus coupons.

Intelligent Features

Everything Needed for Modern Waste Intelligence

Explore our state-of-the-art tools designed for citizens, municipal operators, and environmental analysts.

🔍

AI Waste Scanner

Snap a photo of any item to get instant AI classification (Organic, Plastic, E-Waste, Paper) and optimal disposal guidance.

📊

Smart Bin Monitoring

Real-time ultrasonic sensors continuously measure bin fill levels, temperature, and odor thresholds to prevent overflows.

🗺️

GPS Bin Locator

Interactive city map showing nearest available smart bins, active category types, and turn-by-turn navigation.

🏅

Reward Points & Eco-Credits

Earn points every time you recycle or report waste, redeemable for public transit passes, coffee vouchers, and utility discounts.

🏛️

Municipal Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive fleet management portal for city officials to monitor waste volume trends, truck health, and neighborhood compliance.

🚛

Collection Route Optimization

AI pathfinding algorithms auto-generate optimal collection schedules based on bin priority levels to save time & fuel.

🏆

Community Leaderboard

Gamified neighborhood rankings that encourage friendly competition between wards and communities to achieve zero-waste goals.

🎰

Reverse Vending Machine (RVM)

Automated kiosks that accept plastic bottles and aluminum cans, giving instant digital tokens and digital wallet cashbacks.
